Same board structure as v17. Goal: stop reading as scary by giving the mark a clear personable accent (eye, crest, beak smile) or by abstracting away from the creature entirely toward Vercel/Figma-style geometry.

Why v17 still felt scary Removing the eye made the head read as a faceless dark mass with sharp beak/crest cuts. References stay friendly because they have either a clear face accent (Firefox cat eye, GitHub octocat) or no creature at all (Vercel triangle, Figma dots, GitLab fox crest). v18 explores both directions in 10 families.

Families: A friendly raptor heads with eye · B creature with landscape-inside · C single feather/crest marks · D letter M ligatures · E egg / chick · F cursor + feather · G speech bubble + creature · H geometric mascots · I wings · J abstract symbols.
