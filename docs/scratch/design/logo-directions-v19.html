Revision 19: symbolism, three sources

Direction after v18: 97. Block Stack won; explicit M letterforms are out. Part 1 hybridizes Block Stack’s chip vocabulary with the Figma logo’s soft-primitive grid anatomy, the M only implied (rhythm, valley, twin arches). Part 2 keeps the salvaged explicit-M letterforms for breadth — v18 taught that winners hide in volume. Part 3 is a control group: one agent, empty brief, designing from scratch after studying the repo.
